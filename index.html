How to read it

Every time on this page is a length of recording: how long it would take to play back. Recordings arrive far faster than they play. A whole year of recording arrives about every 14 hours.

1 minute and 1 hour

Each type has its own stream, and wider streams carry more. Every drop is 1 minute of recording, about 10 a second in all. Sixty drops fill a ring: 1 hour of recording.

1 year

Each finished hour flies into the big circle, which holds 1 year of recording: 8,760 hours. It fills clockwise from the top, a new dot every few seconds. The white arc shows how full it is.

The whole dataset

Each small circle is 1 finished year of recording. The ringed space is the year filling now, and the lines show it is the big circle, shrunk to scale.

Illustrative only. Live figures are modelled from average rates, counted on from 6,000,000 hours on 21/10/2026. Waveform names are typical ICU examples standing in for the real types.
