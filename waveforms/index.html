About these figures

Hours of data
Hours on this page measure data, not time on the clock: how long it would take to play back. Data arrives far faster than that, so a full year of recording is added roughly every 14 hours.
Recording hours
Hours of waveform data, with each waveform its own stream. 10 hours of monitoring with 7 waveforms is 70 recording hours.
Patient hours
Hours of monitoring, each counted once however many waveforms are recorded. The same 10 hours is 10 patient hours.
How the count is made
The live figures are modelled from dynamic average rates observed in the real data pipeline.
The five waveforms
  • ECG, electrocardiogram: the heart's electrical activity.
  • ABP, arterial blood pressure: pressure in an artery, measured beat by beat through an arterial line.
  • Pleth, plethysmogram: the pulse of blood volume at the fingertip, from the pulse oximeter.
  • Resp, respiratory rate: the breathing waveform, usually measured as changes in chest impedance.
  • CVP, central venous pressure: pressure in the large veins close to the heart, through a central line.
How it is stored
The underlying data is vast: 0 time-value pairs, stored in an efficient compressed format thanks to the work of the team at 3Lead, who build AtriumDB.

This page does not use real or live patient data, the figures and animations are illustrative.
